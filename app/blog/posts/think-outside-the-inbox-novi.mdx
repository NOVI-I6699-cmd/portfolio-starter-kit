This is what I learned from Google's Course 4 - Think Outside the Inbox, and how I'm applying it to NOVI brand.

### 1. Email is not about selling, it's about trust.
Before this course I thought email was just promos. I learned that a good email provides value first. For NOVI, my plan is 80% value, 20% sell.

### 2. My 3 Core Automations for NOVI
Welcome Series: Welcome + Value + Soft Sell 15% off.
Abandoned Cart: Reminder after 2h + incentive after 24h.
Post-Purchase: Thank you + how to use + ask for review.

### 3. Segmentation
New subscribers, Active buyers, Lapsed customers. Each gets different messaging.

### 4. What I will track
Open Rate >40%, CTR >4%, A/B test subject lines weekly.
